# Related designs (not an exhaustive prior-art search)
Durable asynchronous messaging, public bulletin boards, timestamping, and challenge–response each have established precedents. A shared public information layer is still a communication channel, not an absence of communication. The proposed contribution is a reproducible combination and the isolation/discovery controls. No first-of-its-kind claim is made.
- [RFC 3161 time-stamp protocol](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3161)
- [NIST Interoperable Randomness Beacons](https://csrc.nist.gov/Projects/interoperable-randomness-beacons/beacon-20)
